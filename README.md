## 🤔 What Is This?

Have you ever had a conversation with an AI assistant (like Codex) and wished you could go back and find exactly what was said, what was decided, or what you preferred? This tool is like a **perfect memory box** for your AI chats.

It works quietly on your computer, saving every important part of your conversations into a special local library (called SQLite). You can then search through everything, see beautiful 2D/3D maps of how your topics connect, view a timeline of your discussions, and create backups—all without uploading your data anywhere. **Your data stays on your computer—private and secure.**

## ✨ Main Features

### 🔍 Smart Search (Semantic Search)
This is not just a simple find-and-replace. The tool understands the **meaning** behind your words. For example, if you search for "fix login bug," it will find conversations about "authentication error" or "sign-in issue" because it understands synonyms and context. No more forgetting which chat had the solution!

Imagine a beautiful, colorful map where each bubble represents a topic or idea from your conversations. Lines connect related topics, showing you how your thoughts link together. You can switch between 2D (flat map) and D (spinning 3D globe of your ideas) to explore your knowledge visually. This helps you discover patterns you never noticed before!

### 📅 Interactive Timeline
Scroll through a visual timeline of all your past conversations. See when you discussed specific topics, what decisions were made each day, and how your thinking evolved over time. Perfect for reviewing past projects or remembering why you made a particular choice.

## 🧭 How to Use the Main Dashboard

Once the application is running, you will see a clean, friendly dashboard with several sections. Here is how to navigate:

### 📝 Saving a Conversation
By default, the tool automatically saves conversations as you chat with Codex. However, you can also manually add a conversation: Click the **"Add New"** button, paste in the text of your chat, add a title, and select the date. Press "Save," and it is stored in your memory library.



### 🔎 Searching Your Memory
In the search bar at the top, type any question or phrase. For example: *"What was the best approach for the database issue?"* The tool will instantly show all related conversations, ranked by relevance. You can also filter by date or specific topic tags. Click any result to view the full conversation details.



### 🌐 Viewing the Knowledge Graph
Click the **"Graph"** tab on the left menu. You will see a 2D circle map. Use your mouse to drag around. Scroll to zoom in or out. To switch to the 3D mode, click the "3D" button in the top-right corner. In 3D, you can rotate the whole graph by clicking and dragging. This gives you a spectacular view of how all your ideas connect. Click a bubble to see that topic's details and related conversations.**



### 📅 Viewing the Timeline
Click the **"Timeline"** tab. You will see a horizontal line with dots. Each dot represents a conversation. Click a dot to see what that conversation was about. You can zoom in to specific days or zoom out to see years at a glance. Use the "Play" button to animate through your history chronologically—a fun trip down memory lane!



### 💾 Backing Up Your Data
Click the **"Backup"** tab. You will see two big buttons:"Create Backup" and "Restore Backup." Click "Create Backup," choose a location on your computer (or USB drive) and name the file. The backup will be saved with a .db extension. To restore from a backup on a new computer, click "Restore Backup," select the backup file,and all your saved conversations will return perfectly.
